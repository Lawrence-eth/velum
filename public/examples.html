THE REASON FOR CONFIDENTIAL EXECUTION

Reveal only what payment requires.

InformationOperator / accounting sourceCRE public reportOnchain payment
Invoice reference, vendor ID, PO termsVisibleOmittedOmitted

Request ID, payment commitment, verdict, expiryVisibleVisibleVisible

Contractors need a payment decision. Observers do not need the team’s negotiated terms or remaining purchase-order budget. The confidential handler evaluates those private inputs and releases a request-bound decision.

Privacy has limits: this demo uses synthetic data and local CRE simulation, without enclave protection. The accounting operator sees source data. Public payments and repeated decisions may reveal patterns; commitments do not hide values already published elsewhere. A live confidential deployment is still required for execution privacy.
