WHY CHAINLINK CONFIDENTIAL WORKFLOWS
The agent does not need
your spending rules.
The confidential handler retrieves the source record and API credential, applies deterministic policy, and produces a report bound to the exact payment. The treasury checks the report’s identity, commitment, expiry, and replay state.
Kept out of the report
Vendor identity, invoice reference, purchase order, approval cap, remaining budget, rejection rationale.
Visible onchain
Decision, payment commitment and expiry. Settlement exposes recipient, token and amount. This is policy confidentiality, not anonymous payment.
